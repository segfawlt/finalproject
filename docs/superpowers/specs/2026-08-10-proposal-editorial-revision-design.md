# Proposal Editorial Revision Design

## Purpose

Improve `docs/report/proposal.md` against the supplied proposal structure without changing the
project's scope, claims of planned work, objectives, evaluation thresholds, or reference style.

## Approved Scope

- Preserve the five required sections and their current ordering.
- Preserve the current broadly Harvard-compatible author-year citation and reference formatting.
- Strengthen the critical synthesis in the background literature review by stating what the cited
  tool-use, desired-state, and recovery sources do not establish for privileged Discord mutation.
- Qualify claims about the absence of features in native Discord and proprietary related systems so
  that they are limited to examined or publicly documented workflows.
- Remove only redundant restatements of the intent-interface, approval, validation, and execution
  boundary. The detailed explanation remains in the proposed solution and architecture sections.
- Add a compact traceability table that maps objectives to delivered evidence and assessment
  criteria, using existing objectives and evaluation measures only.

## Exclusions

- Do not add sources, alter the reference list, or apply a different institutional Harvard variant.
- Do not introduce implementation claims, results, or guarantees not already supported by the
  proposal.
- Do not alter the project plan, scope exclusions, assumptions, success thresholds, or objectives.
- Do not restructure the proposal into a new template.

## Edit Locations

1. Section 1.1: replace the categorical native-workflow absence claim with a bounded statement of
   the author's examined workflow.
2. Sections 2.2 to 2.4: add a concise synthesis that distinguishes the support supplied by the
   literature from the safety guarantees the project itself must implement.
3. Section 2.5 and its comparison table: use consistent "publicly documented" wording for product
   capability limitations.
4. Sections 1 to 3: trim duplicate prose only where the safety architecture has already been
   established in more detail.
5. Section 3.5: add an objective-to-evidence traceability table based on O1 to O7 and existing
   evaluation methods.

## Validation

- Confirm every objective O1 to O7 remains unchanged and is represented in the new traceability
  table.
- Confirm no citation or bibliography entry is removed, added, or made inconsistent.
- Read the revised section transitions to ensure the proposal remains coherent and concise.
- Review the diff to ensure changes remain limited to the approved editorial scope.
